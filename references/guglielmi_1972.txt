Guglielmi, N. (1972). Kinetics of deposition of inert particles from an electrolytic bath. Journal of the Electrochemical Society, 119(8), 1009-1012.

Reference summary for Phase III co-deposition model:
- Introduces the two-step successive adsorption mechanism for composite electrodeposition.
- Loose adsorption: reversible, physical (Langmuir), particles covered by adsorbed metal ions.
- Strong adsorption: irreversible, electrochemical, driven by reduction of adsorbed ions on particle surface.
- Only a small fraction θ ≪ σ of loosely adsorbed particles become incorporated.
- Incorporation rate depends on current density, particle concentration, and bath hydrodynamics.

Used in: models/co_deposition.py (GuglielmiCarbonIncorporation class).
