**Priority date:** March 24, 2021 (US Prov. 63/165,502)
**Assignee:** Elektra Steel Co Ltd / Electra Steel Inc
**Inventors:** Ai Quoc Pham, Adolfredo Alvarez, Steven Fatur, Sandeep Nijhawan

**Summary:** Two-step aqueous electrochemical process for producing iron from ore:
1. **Dissolution subsystem** (electrochemical cell 1): Iron-bearing ore is dissolved in acidic aqueous electrolyte to produce an iron-rich solution (Fe²⁺ ions).
2. **Impurity removal:** pH of the iron-rich solution is raised from initial to adjusted pH, precipitating impurities.
3. **Iron plating subsystem** (electrochemical cell 2): Treated iron-rich solution is delivered to a second electrochemical cell where Fe²⁺ is electrochemically reduced to Fe metal.

**Operating conditions disclosed (from abstract/claims):**
- Two-electrochemical-cell architecture (dissolution + plating)
- Acidic aqueous electrolyte for ore dissolution
- pH-controlled impurity removal (raising pH to precipitate contaminants)
- Electrochemical reduction of Fe²⁺ to metallic iron at cathode
- Low-temperature aqueous process (ambient to moderate temperatures, no molten electrolyte)

**CPC classifications:** C25B (electrolytic processes), C25C (electrodeposition), C22B3/00 (wet extraction), C22B3/06 (inorganic acid leaching)

## Key Technical Takeaways for Operating Conditions

From the patent abstracts and metadata:

1. **Process type:** Aqueous electrolysis at low/ambient temperature (not molten salt)
2. **Electrolyte:** Acidic aqueous (likely sulfuric acid-based, given CPC C22B3/06 and PbO₂ anode)
3. **Architecture:** Two-stage electrochemical — (a) anodic dissolution of ore, (b) cathodic deposition of iron
4. **Iron speciation:** Fe²⁺ produced in dissolution step, reduced to Fe⁰ at cathode
5. **Impurity control:** pH swing precipitation between dissolution and plating steps
6. **Anode technology:** Stabilized PbO₂ (Family 3) suggests oxygen evolution at anode in sulfate media
7. **Voltage:** Not explicitly found in available text (Google Patents rate-limited before full claims extraction)
8. **Temperature:** Not explicitly stated but described as "low temperature" vs. traditional blast furnace
