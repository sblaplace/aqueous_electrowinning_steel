The Phase II figures are modelling/data-analysis outputs, not images of a
physical deposit.  The angled-panel map is normalized to applied current but
is not a calibrated current-distribution measurement; see `models/README.md`
for excluded effects and `experiments/data/README.md` for the mass-balance QA
procedure.

Mechanical properties are *screening* predictions (Hall-Petch k=0.5 MPa√m,
solid-solution Ni, dispersion C) anchored to literature compilations; they must
be calibrated with real Vickers, tensile, and EBSD.

Carburization is Fickian screening (D0/Q for γ-Fe, Maynier HV=127+949*C,
Hollomon-Jaffe tempering). Through-carburization flag and case-core composite
strength estimate included.

The adhesion figures are **screening** fracture mechanics, not peel
measurements. No iron peel or coupon-curvature data exists in this repository.
Surface energies are clean-surface literature values and the plastic
amplification factor — the ratio of measured peel work to thermodynamic work of
adhesion — spans an order of magnitude in the literature; the robustness figure
exists precisely to show how much of the verdict rides on it. Marker shape in
the failure-mode map encodes electrical conductivity, so a release coating that
cannot pass current is never read as a viable cathode.

The internal-stress figures are **screening** mechanics predictions, not measured coupon deflections. No iron Stoney or bent-strip curvature data exists in this repository; the Hoffman relaxation distance, saccharin relief constant, chloride shift, and hydrogen absorption fraction are literature-order estimates. The figures exist to demonstrate measurability over instrument resolution floors and to guide the $200 coupon curvature protocol (`internal_stress.coupon_curvature_protocol`).

The cell-architecture figures are a **screening** comparison. Mass-transfer
correlations are literature values measured in other chemistries (ferricyanide,
copper sulfate) and transferred to Fe²⁺ sulfate; equipment costs are engineering
estimates, not vendor quotes. Bar hatching encodes evidence level (solid =
commercially operated, `//` = pilot, `..` = concept) so a proven geometry is
never visually equivalent to an untested one. No architecture in these figures
has been demonstrated for iron by this program.
