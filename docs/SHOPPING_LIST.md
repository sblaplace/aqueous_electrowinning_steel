|---------|-------------|-------|-------|
| **Ferrous Sulfate Heptahydrate 5 lb** | B07BKNB7Y4 | ~$15-20 | Garden grade, fine for initial tests. Lab grade preferred for calibration. |
| **Boric Acid Powder 1 lb** | B01N5OHBH3 | ~$10-15 | Buffer for surface pH control. |
| **Sulfuric acid** | See notes | ~$10-20 | For pH adjustment. Safest option: buy 10% dilute solution. Or use citric acid as safer alternative for starting out. Concentrated (96%) H₂SO₄ requires serious PPE. |

**Note on FeSO₄:** Garden-grade iron sulfate is FeSO₄·7H₂O with minor impurities. Fine for Hull cell screening. Switch to lab-grade (Fisher, Sigma, Carolina Chemical) for calibration experiments where purity matters.

## NOTES

- **Prices fluctuate.** Verify on Amazon before ordering. ASINs may change.
- **Hull cell from Caswell** is direct order, not Amazon. Worth it — it's the industry standard.
- **Nafion membrane** from FuelCellStore.com — not on Amazon. Order the Fumasep FKE-50 as a cheaper alternative if budget is tight.
- **SS sheet** from OnlineMetals.com — they cut to size. Amazon has SS sheet too but often thicker than needed.
- **Citric acid** is a safer alternative to H₂SO₄ for pH adjustment in initial experiments. Available at any grocery store.
